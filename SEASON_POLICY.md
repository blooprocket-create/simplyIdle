# Season Reset and Rewards Policy

## Duration
- Default season length: 28 days.

## Reset Behavior
- Reset season-only metrics:
  - `seasonPoints`
  - seasonal leaderboard rank
- Keep permanent progression:
  - heroes, unlocks, rebirth paths, achievements
  - `highestWaveReached` (lifetime metric)

## Reward Tiers (End of Season)
- Top 1%: exclusive title + banner
- Top 10%: epic banner
- Top 25%: rare banner
- Participation: season emblem

## Anti-Abuse
- Rewards only for accounts with at least:
  - 3 sessions
  - 100 total kills
- Server-side score validation required before final reward lock.
