# Getting Started

<span class="status-badge live">Live</span>

Last reviewed: 2026-04-15

## Account & Save System

- **Offline play** — Core gameplay runs locally with save slots. No account required to play.
- **Online features** — Sign in to unlock social systems, leaderboards, guilds, and cloud saves.
- **Character naming** — Validated and normalized. Public usernames require explicit reservation.

## Your First Session

### 1. Push Waves
Enemies spawn in waves. Clear them to earn **gold**, **EXP**, and **boss tears**. Boss waves appear every 10 waves with bigger rewards.

### 2. Recruit Your Team
Spend gold on **party units** (Squire → Archer Squad → Mage Circle → ...) or summon **heroes** using Boss Tears or Diamonds.

| Unit | Cost | Base DPS |
|------|------|----------|
| Squire | 70 | 2 |
| Archer Squad | 420 | 9 |
| Mage Circle | 3,200 | 48 |
| Cleric Order | 24,000 | 240 |
| Paladin Guard | 230,000 | 1,100 |
| Assassin Cell | 2,400,000 | 7,200 |
| Dragon Riders | 34,000,000 | 55,000 |

Unit costs scale at **×1.15** per purchase.

### 3. Equip Gear
Drops come from wave clears. Equip **Weapon**, **Armor**, and **Accessory** slots. Match gear to your heroes' class for best results.

### 4. Build Facilities
Invest gold into permanent upgrades:

| Facility | Effect per Level |
|----------|-----------------|
| Training | +5% EXP |
| Treasury | +2% Gold |
| Forge | +3% stat multiplier |
| Tactics | +1% power |

### 5. Prestige When You Stall
Once you've pushed to **wave 100**, rebirth becomes available. Each prestige gives a permanent **×1.5 multiplier** and resets your run. See [Prestige & Rebirth](/prestige) for details.

## Early Priorities

- **One carry, one tank** — Keep a reliable damage dealer and a durable frontline hero.
- **Spread upgrades** — Balance facility investment; don't dump everything into one track.
- **Save premium resources** — Don't blow diamonds before your first prestige cycle.
- **Slot balance over rarity** — A balanced loadout beats a single legendary item with empty slots.

## Combat

Combat is fully **idle** — your heroes attack automatically based on their DPS. There is no manual tapping or active combat input. Focus on team composition, equipment, and facility upgrades to increase your damage output.
