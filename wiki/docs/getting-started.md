# Getting Started

## Account and Save

- Core gameplay runs offline with local save slots.
- Online account features power social systems and leaderboard surfaces.
- Character and public naming are validated and normalized.

## First Session Loop

1. Push waves to gather gold and experience.
2. Summon heroes and build a stable team core.
3. Equip upgrades with class-appropriate gear.
4. Spend resources on facilities and permanent tracks.
5. Rebirth when progression stalls to accelerate future runs.

## Early Priorities

- Keep one reliable damage carry and one durable frontline hero.
- Upgrade gear by slot balance, not only rarity.
- Avoid over-spending premium resources before first rebirth cycle.
