# Strategy Guides

<span class="status-badge live">Live</span>

Last reviewed: 2026-04-15

## Early Game — Waves 1–100 (Prestige 0)

### Goals
- Reach wave 100 for your first prestige
- Build a stable clear team before chasing rare summons
- Unlock basic facilities

### Priorities
1. **Spread upgrades evenly** — don't dump everything into one hero
2. **Use free summon charges** — every 10th boss kill gives one
3. **Upgrade facilities early** — the Forge (+1.2% DPS), Library (+0.8% EXP), and Mine (+1% gold) compound over time
4. **Don't grind past wave 100** — prestige immediately for the ×1.5 multiplier
5. **Equip any gear you find** — slot balance matters more than rarity at this stage

### Team Composition
- 1 Warrior or Monk (tank)
- 1–2 DPS (Berserker, Archer, or Mage)
- Fill remaining slots with whatever you pull

## Mid Game — Waves 100–500 (Prestige 1–10)

### Goals
- Prestige efficiently to stack multipliers
- Build a focused team around 2–3 core heroes
- Start ranking heroes and collecting unique weapons

### Priorities
1. **Hero ranking** — rank persists through prestige; levels don't. Invest here.
2. **Class coverage** — multiple classes unlock role-specific passive synergies
3. **Facility maxing before prestige** — facilities carry over, so max them before rebirthing
4. **Spark token economy** — save for Epic+ hero selection (500 tokens)
5. **Push 10–20% past minimum wave** — extra gold/EXP pays off in facility upgrades

### Rebirth Cadence
- Prestige every 15–30 minutes of active play
- Each rebirth should feel noticeably faster than the last
- If you stall below the requirement, gear up instead of grinding

## Late Game — Waves 500+ (Prestige 10+)

### Goals
- Optimize prestige timing for maximum multiplier efficiency
- Pursue Mythic/Godly/Transcendent gear and heroes
- Compete on the leaderboard

### Priorities
1. **Unique weapon ranking** — 12% power per rank, stacks to huge values
2. **Target Legendary+ summons** — use soft pity (starts at pull 20) strategically
3. **Guild participation** — treasury contributions, boss raids, and wars give rewards
4. **VIP progression** — at VIP 10 you have +30% damage, +25% gold, +25% EXP
5. **Achievement hunting** — each achievement gives +3% power (up to +75% total)

### Prestige Math
At prestige 15, you need wave 548. At prestige 20, wave 965. The climb is exponential — every prestige matters more.

$$
\text{Total multiplier at } p = 1.5^p
$$

## Economy Tips

| Resource | Best Source | Don't Waste On |
|----------|------------|----------------|
| Gold | Wave clears + Mine facility | Over-leveling weak heroes |
| Shards | Shard recycling + gifts | Ranking Common heroes past rank 3 |
| Diamonds | VIP rewards + achievements | Random summons without pity built up |
| Boss Tears | Boss kills (1 per kill) | — |
| Spark Tokens | Duplicate summons | Buying free charges (50 tokens) |
| Essence | Gifts + VIP | — |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Grinding past prestige threshold** — the ×1.5 multiplier is almost always worth the reset
2. **Ignoring facilities** — they're permanent and compound every run
3. **Ranking Common heroes** — shard costs scale with rarity multiplier; save for Epic+
4. **Neglecting equipment slots** — three decent items > one great item + two empty slots
5. **Skipping achievements** — free +3% power each, permanently
