# Strategy Guides

## Early Game: Waves 1 to 50

- Build a stable clear baseline before chasing rare pulls.
- Spread upgrades across team and facilities for consistency.
- Push for first rebirth threshold with minimal premium spend.

## Mid Game: Waves 50 to 250

- Shift from survival-only to balanced damage plus economy.
- Tighten class role placement for formation bonuses.
- Convert stalled runs into rebirth acceleration earlier.

## Late Game and Prestige Cycles

- Optimize around permanent track efficiency.
- Re-evaluate gear and hero composition each prestige band.
- Use event and social reward loops to smooth resource spikes.
