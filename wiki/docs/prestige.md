# Prestige & Rebirth

<span class="status-badge live">Live</span>

Last reviewed: 2026-04-15

## How Prestige Works

When your wave progression stalls, **rebirth** lets you reset your current run in exchange for a permanent power multiplier. Each prestige cycle makes subsequent runs dramatically faster.

## Rebirth Requirements

The minimum wave to unlock rebirth scales with your prestige count:

$$
\text{Required Wave}(p) = \lceil 100 \times 1.12^p \rceil
$$

| Prestige | Required Wave |
|----------|---------------|
| 0 → 1 | 100 |
| 1 → 2 | 112 |
| 2 → 3 | 126 |
| 3 → 4 | 141 |
| 5 → 6 | 177 |
| 10 → 11 | 311 |
| 15 → 16 | 548 |
| 20 → 21 | 965 |

## Rebirth Multiplier

Each prestige grants a flat **×1.5 multiplier** to your overall power. This stacks multiplicatively:

| Prestiges | Total Multiplier |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | 1.5× |
| 2 | 2.25× |
| 3 | 3.375× |
| 5 | 7.59× |
| 10 | 57.7× |
| 15 | 437.9× |
| 20 | 3,325× |

## What Resets

- Current wave progress → back to wave 1
- Gold → reset to starting amount
- Party unit levels → reset
- Hero EXP → reset

## What Persists

- **Prestige multiplier** — permanent and cumulative
- **Heroes** — your roster stays (levels reset, hero rank stays)
- **Equipment** — all gear is kept
- **Facilities** — permanent upgrades carry over
- **Shards, diamonds, boss tears** — currencies persist
- **Achievements** — permanent
- **Social progress** — friends, guild, leaderboard

## When to Prestige

### Early Game (Prestige 0–5)
Rebirth as soon as you hit the minimum wave. Don't grind past the requirement — the multiplier payoff is worth the reset.

### Mid Game (Prestige 5–15)
Push 10–20% beyond the minimum wave before rebirthing. The extra gold and EXP from higher waves compounds into stronger facility upgrades.

### Late Game (Prestige 15+)
The required wave climbs steeply. Focus on hero ranking, unique weapons, and equipment optimization between prestiges. Each rebirth should feel like a meaningful power spike.

## Prestige Optimization Tips

1. **Max facilities before rebirthing** — They carry over and accelerate the next run.
2. **Rank up heroes** — Hero rank persists; hero levels don't. Invest in permanent power.
3. **Time your prestiges** — If you're stalling hard below the requirement, consider upgrading gear first.
4. **Use achievements** — Each unlocked achievement gives +3% power (up to +75%). Chase easy ones before prestige.
