# VIP Program

<span class="status-badge live">Live</span>

Last reviewed: 2026-04-15

## Overview

The VIP system rewards cumulative engagement with 10 tiers of permanent bonuses. Points are earned through gameplay milestones and progress toward higher tiers.

## VIP Tiers

| Level | Points Required | Diamonds | Gold | Shards | Essence |
|-------|----------------|----------|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | 50 | 50 | 1,200 | 50 | 0 |
| 2 | 150 | 100 | 2,800 | 90 | 1 |
| 3 | 350 | 180 | 5,200 | 140 | 1 |
| 4 | 700 | 300 | 9,200 | 220 | 2 |
| 5 | 1,500 | 500 | 16,000 | 340 | 3 |
| 6 | 3,000 | 800 | 30,000 | 500 | 4 |
| 7 | 6,500 | 1,250 | 52,000 | 760 | 6 |
| 8 | 15,000 | 2,000 | 90,000 | 1,100 | 9 |
| 9 | 35,000 | 3,200 | 145,000 | 1,550 | 13 |
| 10 | 100,000 | 5,000 | 220,000 | 2,200 | 20 |

Points are **cumulative** — reaching VIP 3 requires 350 total points, not 350 on top of VIP 2.

## Per-Level Bonuses

Each VIP level grants stacking permanent bonuses:

| Bonus | Per Level |
|-------|-----------|
| Damage | +3% |
| Gold | +2.5% |
| EXP | +2.5% |

At **VIP 10**, you have +30% damage, +25% gold, and +25% EXP permanently.

## VIP 3+ Perk: Summon Discount

Starting at VIP 3, hero summoning costs are reduced by **10%**:
- Boss Tears summon: 500 → **450**
- Diamond summon: 500 → **450**

## Profile Badges

VIP status shows on your public profile:
- **VIP 1–9:** "VIP Member" badge
- **VIP 10:** "Elite Patron" badge
